1. Summary

Competitor Radar already scores a site and its competitors every day, tracks fixes, and reads Search Console. This plan turns it into a subscription product with a free tier, live AI-assistant tracking, and one-click fixes on the customer's own website.

What we ask the reviewers to decide. The technical choices in section 15 (worker platform, queue, key management, v1 integration scope) and the commercial choices (legal entity and GST set-up, final prices, AI cost cap, payment gateway for foreign customers).

The three things that matter most.

  1. AI query cost is the main financial risk. If the cost per AI query doubles and prices fall 20%, the base case drops from ₹1.39L a month to a loss. Plan limits, shared caching and a hard monthly cost cap are part of the design, not later polish.
  2. Auto-fix is the differentiator and the largest liability. It must be approval-first, reversible and logged. Section 8 sets the rules.
  3. The crawler is not ready for paying strangers. It has no protection against being pointed at internal addresses, and it runs inside time-limited serverless functions. Both are fixed in the first three weeks.

Recommendation. Build in this order: billing and hardening, then AI tracking, then alerts, and launch paid at week 8. Add integrations after launch, starting with Cloudflare because it needs no marketplace approval. Raise Growth to ₹3,499 and Agency to ₹12,999 if early customer interviews allow it (section 13.4).

5. Target architecture

The web app stays on Vercel. Heavy and long work moves to workers. Postgres remains the queue and the source of truth until volume proves otherwise.

Design decisions

  • Shared crawl cache. A competitor domain is crawled once a day no matter how many customers track it. This lowers cost and builds the daily history of every domain, which a new rival cannot backfill.
  • Workers pull jobs with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED. This lets several workers run safely. If volume outgrows Postgres, move to a managed queue (Inngest, Trigger.dev or QStash) without changing the job shapes.
  • Entitlements are computed on the server from the active subscription row, and checked in every API route and in the worker before spending money.
  • Raw snapshots go to object storage (Supabase Storage or Cloudflare R2). Postgres keeps scores, summaries and indexes only.
  • Provider abstraction for search and AI engines, so a price rise or policy change at one vendor means a config change.

Sizing, base case (month 12)

About 280 paid sites, roughly 1,500 distinct paid-tracked domains after sharing, plus about 860 free-tier crawls a day. At an assumed 45 seconds per crawl and 10 concurrent crawls per worker, this is 2 to 3 small workers. AI probes: about 46,600 queries a month, spread across the day to stay under provider rate limits.

7. Live AI visibility tracking

Today's AI score checks signals on the site. The paid feature asks AI assistants real questions and records who they recommend.

How it works

  1. Claude Haiku turns the site's keywords and locations into 10 to 50 buyer-style prompts, for example "best turnkey interior contractor in Vadodara". The customer can edit them.
  2. A worker sends each prompt to the plan's engines through their official APIs with web search switched on: OpenAI, Gemini with Google Search grounding, Perplexity Sonar, and Claude with web search.
  3. A parser records which brands and domains appear, in what position, with what sentiment, and which URLs are cited.
  4. Daily roll-ups give mention rate, share of voice against tracked competitors, and the sources the engines trust.

Rules that keep it honest

  • Answers vary between runs. Report a mention rate over repeated runs, never a single yes or no.
  • API answers can differ from what a person sees in the consumer app, where personalisation applies. Say this on the screen.
  • Identical prompt, engine and location requests are cached for 24 hours across customers.
  • Each provider's terms on storing and displaying answers must be checked. Google, for example, may require showing its search suggestions beside grounded answers.
  • A monthly query budget per plan is enforced before each call. A global daily spend cap stops the queue if cost runs ahead of plan.

Spike first

Wix API coverage for SEO fields is the least certain. Run a three-day spike before committing the 3 person-weeks. The same applies to Yoast and Rank Math fields over REST.

Safety rules for every automatic change

  1. Propose. The tool lists the exact change and the page.
  2. Preview. The customer sees before and after.
  3. Approve. Only a signed-in owner or admin can approve. Nothing is applied without a click.
  4. Apply and snapshot. The previous value is stored before writing.
  5. Verify. The next scan confirms the change is live and the page still loads.
  6. Undo. One click restores the snapshot. Every step is in the audit log.

v1 limits itself to low-risk, reversible items: meta tags, structured data, redirects, llms.txt and robots.txt. It does not rewrite page copy automatically. Access is requested with the narrowest scopes each platform offers, tokens are encrypted, and the customer can disconnect at any time.

9. Billing and tax

Razorpay Subscriptions flow

  1. Create one Razorpay plan per tier and billing period (monthly and annual).
  2. The customer starts checkout from the billing page. Cards and UPI AutoPay are supported.
  3. Webhooks drive state: subscription.authenticated, activated, charged, pending, halted, cancelled, completed, and payment.failed. Every webhook is verified by HMAC signature and processed once, keyed by event id.
  4. The subscriptions row is the single source for entitlements. A failed payment starts a 7-day grace period with reminder emails, then the account drops to Free. Data is kept, features lock.
  5. Upgrades take effect at once with the price difference charged. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal.

GST and invoicing

  • 18% GST on software subscriptions. Gujarat customers pay CGST 9% plus SGST 9%. Customers in other states pay IGST 18%. The place of supply comes from the customer's billing state.
  • A sequential invoice per payment, with the customer's GSTIN when given. E-invoicing becomes mandatory above the turnover threshold. Confirm the current threshold and the registration timing with a chartered accountant.
  • Annual plans are prepaid. Revenue is recognised monthly and the unearned part sits as deferred revenue.
  • Payment fees are about 2% plus GST on the fee, so about 2.8% of the ex-GST price. The GST on the fee is claimable as input credit.

Customers outside India

Razorpay can take international cards if enabled on the account. A merchant-of-record provider such as Paddle or Lemon Squeezy handles foreign sales tax for a higher fee (commonly around 5% plus a fixed amount). Decide this before opening to non-Indian users.

For the CTO

  1. Which legal entity bills customers, and GST registration timing
  2. Final prices: base set or option B, and the annual discount
  3. Monthly AI cost cap as a share of revenue
  4. Gateway for non-Indian customers: Razorpay international or a merchant of record

  7. Foreign exchange policy, since most costs are in dollars and revenue is in rupees
